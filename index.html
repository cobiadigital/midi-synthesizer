About Poly Synth

An eight-voice subtractive synthesizer that runs entirely in your browser. Two oscillators, a sub and noise into a four-pole ladder filter with drive, two envelopes, an LFO, an arpeggiator, and a stereo delay and reverb. The panel follows a Korg Minilogue; the filter follows a Moog.

Playing it

  • Press Start audio first. Browsers will not make a sound before you ask.
  • Plug in a MIDI keyboard, tap the on-screen keys, or play A through K on a computer keyboard.
  • Turn a knob and the address bar follows. Share sends that patch as a link and a picture.

Installing it

On iPhone, tap the browser's Share button and then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Install app. It runs full screen and keeps working offline once loaded.
